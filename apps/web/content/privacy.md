ValueSkins is run by Valueskins Private Limited. This page explains what data ValueSkins collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

## What we collect

- **From Instagram, when you sign in with Instagram:** your Instagram user id, username, name, profile picture, account type, follower count, following count, post count, and an engagement rate that ValueSkins calculates from the like and comment counts of your latest 12 posts. We also keep the access token Instagram gives us, stored encrypted.
- **From you:** your email address, whether you are a creator or a brand, and billing details if you are a brand.
- **For payments:** creators give their bank and identity details to Razorpay, which stores them. ValueSkins does not store PAN or full bank account numbers.
- **On your device:** a session cookie that keeps you signed in.
- **Deal records:** deals, applications, payments, invoices, emails sent about deals, and messages you submit, such as feedback and disputes.

## How we use it

- To sign you in and keep you signed in.
- To show your Instagram profile to brands when you apply to a deal.
- To run deals: applications, payments, invoices and notifications by email.
- To keep records that the law requires.

## Who we share it with

- Brands see a creator's Instagram profile details when the creator applies to their deal. Creators see the brand's name on the deal.
- Service providers that run ValueSkins: Razorpay (payments), Resend (email), Render and Vercel (hosting).
- We do not sell your data.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is active. Invoices, payments and deal records are kept as a legal record after an account is deleted.

## Deleting your data

- Sign in and use **Delete my account** on your account page. We remove your name, email, username and Instagram details, end your sessions, and delete your tokens. Deals, payments and invoices are kept as a legal record and show "Deleted user".
- Or remove ValueSkins from your Instagram settings (Settings, Apps and websites). Instagram then tells ValueSkins, and we delete the stored Instagram access token.
- Or ask Instagram to send us a data deletion request. We delete your account details as above and give you a confirmation code and a page where you can check the status.
- Or email us at valueskinsfounder@gmail.com.
